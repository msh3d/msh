About MSH Studio

Founder · Mumbai, India

MSH Studio is a suite of Rhino 3D plugins for jewellery designers and manufacturers, built by Avanish, Founder, MSH 3D Studio, in Mumbai, India.

Why it exists

After 12+ years in jewellery product development and CAD, I kept solving the same production problems by hand: pavé layouts, azure cutters, sweeps, metal weights and client images. These plugins automate them.

The suite

  • Azure Cutter Pro: honeycomb undercuts from a pavé layout.
  • Auto Pave Pro: Gradient and Scattered pavé on any surface.
  • Paveplanner Pro: interactive multi-zone pavé planning.
  • Super Smart Sweep: surface-aligned sweeps with twist and taper.
  • Metal Reports & Pro Capture: metal weight, multi-metal cost, diamond report and 8K capture to PowerPoint.

Requirements

Rhino 8 for Windows.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

MSH Studio is operated by Avanish as an individual proprietor (“MSH 3D Studio”, “we”) in Mumbai, India. This policy explains what we collect and why.

Data we collect

  • Name and email address, when you request a trial, subscribe or contact us.
  • Rhino serial number, to verify trial eligibility and license ownership.
  • Machine ID, to issue and validate machine-locked licenses.
  • Messages and files you send us for support or customisation requests.

We do not collect usage telemetry from the plugins.

How we use it

  • To issue, verify and support licenses and trials.
  • To send invoices, renewal reminders and product updates you need.
  • To prevent unauthorised use or sharing of licenses.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only with service providers needed to run licensing, payments and email, or where required by law.

Retention and your rights

We keep licensing and invoice records for as long as needed for support and legal or tax purposes. You may request access, correction or deletion by emailing msh3dstudio@gmail.com.

Third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and videos from YouTube. Those providers may process technical data under their own policies.
